# Run the frozen dynamic mechanism experiment

> 历史记录的脱敏摘录；不是当前授权或当前完成状态。原件未改写。

The campaign uses only `conv-30/41/42`. Each conversation has 12 source-based author questions and at most 12 deterministic official QA selections. Missing QA categories are recorded in `plan.json`; remaining places come from the other categories. The five arms share the same Build and initial retrieval. Three repetitions produce at most 1,080 logical answers. Model calls include construction, analysis, resolution, answers, failures, and bounded retries.
